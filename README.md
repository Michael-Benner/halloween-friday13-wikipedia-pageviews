# Halloween, Friday the 13th, and Wikipedia Page Views

**Michael Benner**

## Research Question

My project investigates which categories of English Wikipedia articles experience the largest increases in readership around Halloween and Friday the 13th, and how the patterns differ between these dates. The study will examine articles related to horror, supernatural topics, true crime, folklore and superstition, and historical disasters. Using daily page-view data from the Wikimedia Pageviews API, the project will compare article readership on Halloween and Friday the 13th to typical baseline readership levels. The goal is to identify whether culturally significant and supposedly "spooky" dates are associated with measurable changes in what people search for and read on Wikipedia.

## Data

Data will be collected from the Wikimedia Pageviews API. The dataset will contain daily page-view counts for twelve English Wikipedia articles between January 1, 2016 and December 31, 2025.

## Methods

Python will be used to collect data from the Wikimedia Pageviews API. The analysis will compare page views on Halloween with typical October dates and page views on Friday the 13th with other Fridays in the same month. Percentage changes from baseline readership will be calculated and compared across categories.
